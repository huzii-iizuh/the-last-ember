==============================================================
  THE LAST EMBER  -  THE EASY GUIDE
  "Even the smallest light can change the dark."
==============================================================

This file explains the whole game in simple words: what it is,
how to play it, how to share it, what it is made of, and how
all the pieces fit together.


--------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS THIS GAME?
--------------------------------------------------------------

The Last Ember is a short puzzle-platform game.

You are a tiny living flame - the last fire left in a world
that went dark. You travel left to right through ruins at night
to reach an old lighthouse and light it again.

The world is 3D, but you move like a 2D game: left, right and
jump. This style is called "2.5D".

The game has 3 big ideas:

  FIRE    - you ARE the fire. Your glow lights up the dark.
  SHADOW  - some stone paths are hidden in the dark. When your
            light gets close, they appear and you can stand on
            them. Violet shadow thorns hurt you.
  TIME    - your special power, the "Time Pulse", freezes moving
            dangers for a few seconds so you can get past.

The level has 4 parts:
  1. The Awakening ...... learn to move and jump
  2. The Hidden Path .... your light reveals hidden stones
  3. The Frozen Passage . freeze a spinning blade and a crusher
  4. The Lighthouse ..... everything together, then the ending

At the end, the lighthouse lights up, its beams sweep the sky,
and every street lamp in the world turns back on, one by one.


--------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO PLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------

Start the game:
  - Double-click  build\TheLastEmber.exe
  - Click PLAY.

Keys:
  Move left / right ... A and D (or the arrow keys)
  Jump ................ Space (or W, or Up arrow)
  Time Pulse .......... Q (or right mouse button)
  Back to last shrine . R
  Pause ............... Esc

Tips:
  - Blue-marked things can be frozen with the Time Pulse.
  - The Time Pulse lasts 2.5 seconds, then needs 3 seconds to
    recharge. The bar in the bottom-left shows when it is ready.
  - Little floating sparks show where a hidden stone is.
  - Fire shrines are checkpoints. If you fall, you come back to
    the last shrine you lit.


--------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO SHARE THE GAME WITH A FRIEND
--------------------------------------------------------------

The whole game is ONE file: build\TheLastEmber.exe (about 105 MB).
Your friend does NOT need to install anything else.

  Step 1. Right-click TheLastEmber.exe -> Send to ->
          Compressed (zipped) folder.
  Step 2. Upload the zip to Google Drive, OneDrive or
          WeTransfer, and send your friend the link.
  Step 3. Tell your friend: when Windows says
          "Windows protected your PC", click
          "More info" then "Run anyway".
          (The game is not "signed", which costs money.
          It is safe.)
  Step 4. The first start can take up to a minute.

It is also on GitHub: download it from the "Releases" page of
the the-last-ember repository.

What your friend's PC needs:
  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  - A graphics card that supports DirectX 12
  - 4 GB of memory and 150 MB of space

--------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT THE GAME IS MADE WITH (THE "TECH STACK")
--------------------------------------------------------------

  Godot 4.7.2 ........ The game engine. Free and open source.
  GDScript ........... The programming language (like Python).
  Blender 5.2 ........ 3D modelling. Every model (the flame,
                       ruins, lighthouse, lamps, blades) was
                       made by a small Blender script.
  DirectX 12 ......... The Windows graphics system.
  Jolt Physics ....... Makes collisions and falling work.
  Sound .............. Every sound was created by code, from math.

No downloaded art was used in this game - everything was made
for it.


--------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW THE FILES ARE ORGANISED (THE "HIERARCHY")
--------------------------------------------------------------

  main.tscn ........ The front door. The game starts here.
  project.godot .... Settings and controls.

  scripts\ ......... The BRAINS (all the code).
      main.gd             runs the game: menu, camera, ending
      player.gd           the flame: moving, jumping, Time Pulse
      level.gd            checkpoints, ending light, background
      freezable.gd        anything the Time Pulse can freeze
      hidden_platform.gd  stones that appear in your light
      shrine.gd           the fire shrines (checkpoints)
      lamp.gd             street lamps that relight at the end
      lighthouse.gd       the lighthouse and its beams
      kill_area.gd        anything that hurts you
      trigger.gd          story messages and the ending trigger
      layout.gd           the level layout written as numbers
      ui.gd               menus and on-screen messages
      sfx.gd              sounds and music

  scenes\ .......... The BUILDING BLOCKS.
      level.tscn          the whole level
      player.tscn         the flame
      props\              shrine, lamp, blade, crusher, moving
                          platform, thorns, lighthouse, hidden
                          stone

  ui\ .............. Menus and their look.
  models\ .......... 3D shapes.
  materials\ ....... What surfaces look like (stone, metal).
  audio\ ........... All sound files.
  tools\ ........... Helper scripts used to BUILD the game.
  art\ ............. The original Blender file.
  build\ ........... The finished game (.exe).


--------------------------------------------------------------
7. HOW THE GAME IS PUT TOGETHER (SCENES)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Godot builds games from "scenes" - groups of parts that can hold
other scenes inside them, like Lego.

  Main  (runs everything)
   |
   +-- World
   |     +-- Lighting and fog
   |     +-- The Moon
   |     +-- Camera (follows the flame from the side)
   |     +-- Level
   |     |     +-- Platforms (stone pillars)
   |     |     +-- Props (hidden stones, shrines, lamps, lighthouse)
   |     |     +-- Hazards (blades, crusher, moving platform, thorns)
   |     |     +-- Signs (tutorial words)
   |     |     +-- Triggers (story text and the ending)
   |     |     +-- Ruins (background)
   |     |     +-- Far Layer (distant ruins, far lighthouse, moon)
   |     +-- Player (the flame)
   |
   +-- Sound and music
   +-- Menus and messages (on top)

The flame is made of:
  - an invisible ball shape (for touching the ground)
  - 3 flame shapes that flicker
  - a soft glow and a real light that lights up the stones
  - a trail of sparks


--------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE RULES AND NUMBERS
--------------------------------------------------------------

  Speed ................ 6 metres per second
  Jump ................. about 2.2 metres high, 4 metres long
  Time Pulse ........... freezes things within 7 metres
                         lasts 2.5 s, recharges in 3 s
  Hidden stones ........ appear when you are within 3.5 metres
  Falling .............. below the level = back to last shrine

To change a number, open scripts\player.gd and change the value
at the top.


--------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW THE MAIN PARTS WORK (SIMPLE VERSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------

FREEZING (TIME)
  Every moving danger has its own little clock. Normally the
  clock ticks and the danger moves. The Time Pulse simply stops
  that clock for 2.5 seconds. When it starts again, the danger
  continues from exactly where it stopped.

HIDDEN STONES (SHADOW)
  A hidden stone is see-through and you fall through it. Every
  moment it checks: "is the flame close?" If yes, it fades in,
  becomes solid, and stays.

CHECKPOINTS
  When you touch a fire shrine it lights up and remembers its
  place. If you die, you come back there and every danger
  resets.

THE ENDING (FIRE)
  When you reach the lighthouse, its lamp lights up and the
  camera pulls back. Then every street lamp and shrine relights
  in order, starting near the lighthouse and spreading back
  across the world. The music turns warm.

--------------------------------------------------------------
11. HOW TO MAKE A NEW .EXE
--------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open the project in Godot.
  2. Click Project -> Export...
  3. Choose "Windows Desktop" and click "Export Project".
  4. Save it as build\TheLastEmber.exe

Before sharing a new version: play the whole level once, test
dying and coming back, click every menu button, and open the new
.exe once.


--------------------------------------------------------------
12. TESTING
--------------------------------------------------------------

The game has a hidden test mode that takes a picture and closes:

  TheLastEmber.exe -- --shot=picture.png --at=66 --pulse

  --at=66   start the flame 66 metres into the level
  --pulse   use the Time Pulse
  --end     play the ending

What was tested: every part of the level, the Time Pulse, the
ending and the .exe. What still needs real players: whether the
jumps feel fair and fun.
